# Privacy Policy for Birdly

**Last Updated:** January 2026

## Introduction

Birdly ("we," "our," or "the app") is an open source educational application designed to help users learn bird identification through interactive games. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

## Open Source

Birdly is an open source application. The source code is publicly available for review, audit, and contribution. This transparency ensures that you can verify our privacy practices directly.

## Data Collection and Storage

### We Do Not Collect Any Personal Information

Birdly is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not:

- Require account creation or login
- Collect email addresses, names, or any contact information
- Track your location
- Collect device identifiers
- Use analytics or tracking services
- Integrate with third-party analytics or advertising services
- Transmit any data to external servers

### Local Data Storage Only

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

1. **Learning Progress**: Completion percentages for birds and images you've practiced
2. **App Preferences**: Your sound and haptic feedback settings
3. **App State**: Whether you've seen the splash screen (to avoid showing it repeatedly)

This data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework and remains entirely on your device. It is never transmitted, shared, or accessible to us or any third parties.

### Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app from your device. This will remove all locally stored progress and preferences.

## Third-Party Services

Birdly does not use any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data processors. The app operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection to function.

## Children's Privacy

Birdly does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is suitable for all ages and complies with applicable children's privacy laws.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy will reflect any changes. Your continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. All data stored locally on your device can be removed by uninstalling the app.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through:

- The app's open source repository: [https://github.com/anthonyc-r/birdly]

## Compliance

This privacy policy complies with:

- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
- Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
- Apple App Store privacy requirements

---

**Summary:** Birdly does not collect, track, or transmit any personal information. All data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.
